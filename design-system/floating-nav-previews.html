Translucent capsule with backdrop blur — the dot-grid shows faintly through. This is the iOS 26 Liquid Glass pattern Apple Music, Photos, and Notes all adopted. Very modern; less warm. iOS is free; Android needs RenderEffect.createBlurEffect (API 31+) with a translucent fallback below.
