Releaf — Scan-first prototypes

Three directions for making Scan the gravitational center of the home experience, ranging from a complete IA rethink to a more conservative hero-card treatment. All mocks use real Releaf brand tokens (greens, cream, amber import accent) pulled from the iOS theme.
