Same Scan hero, but with a category grid beneath it: tap "Letter" to launch the camera with that tag pre-applied. Solves the real friction of filing after scanning. The most-used tag (Letter) is highlighted in amber. Great for users who scan a steady stream of mail or paperwork.

Built for the case that breaks single-shot scanners: multi-page docs. The persistent stack counter (top right) and batch summary (bottom) make it obvious the user is building one document. The shutter has a "+" subscript so it always reads as "add another page". Auto-capture mode means hands-free scanning of a 10-page contract.
