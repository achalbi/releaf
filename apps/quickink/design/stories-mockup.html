What's different — v1, v2, v3

v1 · memories-style v2 · chapbook v3 · refined memories
Concept Curated album with items A small bound book of pages Same as v1, sanded down
Stories tab Horizontal suggestion row + cards Shelf of book covers + banner with three angles One focused suggestion card with full reason text + dismiss
Cover treatment Hero photo with title overlay (always coral fallback) Composed cover — Q monogram, serif title, coral rule Auto-pulls dominant color from hero; one-tap typographic cream alt
Suggestion preview Grid of item thumbnails Three angles to pick (food / daily / receipts) Sample first page rendered, item strip below
Editor Card list with inline icon row in slots Single page canvas with page strip + voice clip Single "+" button per slot, per-item menu, active card elevation
Reader Cover + scrolling feed Book pages, swipe to turn, drop cap + pull quote Sticky day markers ("May 4 · evening"), end card with reply + make-your-own
Share sheet Four options + an awkward fifth tile Four options + in-app feed deferred Preview thumbnail at top, inline passcode option on public-link
Brand fit Generic photo-app pattern Unmistakably QuickInk — paper, ink, calligraphic Q Editorial polish layered onto a familiar shape
Build cost ~4 weeks for MVP ~6 weeks for MVP (page-turn, voice infra, ToC) ~4 weeks for MVP (same as v1, plus 1 week of polish)
See design/STORIES_DESIGN.md for the full plan. v3 is the recommended path forward — it preserves what works about v1 while addressing the brand-fit and clarity issues, without the engineering cost of the v2 chapbook reinvention.
