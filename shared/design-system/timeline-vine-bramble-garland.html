Activity timeline · bramble garland

Continuous serpentine vine on the left, flower at each entry, leaves and berries riding the vine between entries. Heaviest of the timeline-vine variants.

Marker palette Coral — today / featured Green — routine Yellow — highlight Dry — archival
Featured entries (coral, yellow) carry the larger flower (rx=3.8, ry=5.5). Routine entries (green, dry) carry the smaller flower (rx=3.4, ry=5).

Design prompt

Design an "activity timeline" component for the Releaf journaling app
(iOS SwiftUI + Android Jetpack Compose, warm editorial design system).

CARD
- 320pt wide, cream fill #FFFAF4, rounded corners 14pt, 0.5pt border #463C311F
- Eyebrow label "Activity" (11pt, weight 500, color #5F5245) at top-left
  with hairline divider below (0.5pt #463C311F)
- ~46pt of left padding before the vine begins (the vine is decoration,
  not a flush edge marker)

TIMELINE ENTRIES
- Five entries vertically, ~90pt apart, starting around y=140 inside the card
- Each entry has three text rows, all left-aligned at x=80:
  * date (11pt regular, color #5F5245) — e.g. "Today", "Mar 22"
  * title (14pt weight 500, color #463C31) — e.g. "Journaled 3 entries"
  * preview (12pt regular, color #5F5245) — single-line description

VINE (the signature decoration)
- A continuous serpentine stem in deep leaf green #5B8C52, 1.8pt stroke,
  rounded end caps
- Vertical center line at x=46 inside the card
- Between each pair of entries the vine alternates Q-bezier bulges:
  right (control x=66) and left (control x=26), giving a gentle wave
- Total horizontal column claimed by vine + decoration ~50pt

ENTRY MARKERS — flowers only at each entry
- 5-petal flower (5 ellipses rotated at 72-deg increments around the center)
  with a small contrasting circular center
- Featured / large blooms (coral and yellow): petals rx=3.8 ry=5.5,
  stroke 0.4, center radius 2.4
- Routine / smaller blooms (green and dry brown): petals rx=3.4 ry=5
- Color hierarchy:
  * coral #E07856 fill, #C65A3E stroke, #F4C430 center -> "today" / featured
  * green #7AA874 fill, #5B8C52 stroke, #F4C430 center -> routine
  * yellow #F4C430 fill, #E8B923 stroke, #C65A3E center -> highlight
  * dry  #B8956A fill, #8B7355 stroke, #F4C430 center -> archival

GARLAND — leaves and berries only BETWEEN flowers, never at an entry
- At each vine apex (the four bulge tips between five entries), place:
  1. ONE stout almond-shaped leaf attached to the apex, angled outward
     ~40deg (right bulges rotate +40, left bulges rotate -40)
     - path: M0,0 Q-4.5,-9 0,-17 Q4.5,-9 0,0 Z
     - fill #7AA874, stroke #5B8C52 0.5, midrib line #463C3166 0.4
  2. ONE plump berry trio sitting ON the vine line itself (not floating
     beside it), radii 2.6 / 2.4 / 2.2 in a tight cluster
     - coral #C65A3E on right bulges, brown #8B7355 on left bulges
     - offsets from cluster center: (0,0), (3.8, 2.4), (-1.8, 3.6)

TONE
- Warm, hand-drawn, editorial — pressed botanicals in a journal margin
- Heaviest of the timeline-vine variants; vine is decoration first,
  navigation second
- Flat fills with thin contrasting strokes for definition; no gradients,
  no drop shadows
