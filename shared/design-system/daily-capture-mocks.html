How to read these

Mock 1 (Home) is the proposed primary surface — Today's NotepadEntry editor inline, not behind a "Today →" tap. Mock 2 (Daily Log) is the same shape applied to any other date, reached via day strip, calendar, deep link, or notification. Mock 3 (QuickCaptureSheet) is the universal capture entry, opened from the floating Leaf button. The five tiles map 1:1 to the capture kinds the schema already supports.

Visual elements pulled from design-tokens.json: cream canvas #F5EEE3, dot grid at 22 px, coral accent #E77850, Newsreader for serif headings, Manrope for everything else, floating cream-card BottomNav with the coral FAB at +8 dp elevation.
