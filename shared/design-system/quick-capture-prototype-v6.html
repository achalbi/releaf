Where the height went

v5 fit by shrinking the Scan hero from 200 px to 130 px. v6 puts those 70 px back and finds them in three other places — the chip-row pre-tags (already there in v5), tighter capture-tile aspect ratio (1.05 → 1.1, saves ~16 px), a smaller Home backdrop above the sheet (78 px → 70 px, gives back 8 px), and tighter padding throughout (saves another ~10 px).

v6 vs v5 — only what changed
  • Scan hero restored to full v4 dimensions (~200 px) — title back to 30 px serif, original padding, paper at 78×96, corner brackets at 110×130.
  • Capture tile aspect ratio: 1.05 → 1.1 — slightly wider than tall now. Saves ~16 px across the two-row grid. Icon chips, labels, and hints unchanged.
  • Home backdrop tightened: 78 px → 70 px above the sheet — gives back 8 px. The status bar + the "TUESDAY · April 28" header still fit comfortably above the scrim.
  • Divider margin tightened by 2 px.

Same trade-offs as v5

The pre-tag chip row still hides tags 4–6 past the visible edge unless the user scrolls horizontally. The "scroll for more →" hint above signals it. If 30+ tags show up in real usage, the row needs a "More tags" affordance, but that's a future call.

iPhone SE (375 × 667) still doesn't fit even at v6 sizing — only ~580 px would be available there. Same fallback applies: sheet starts higher, or the small-screen mode swaps to the Scan | Capture tab alternative from v4's open questions.

Implementation footprint

Identical to v5 — pre-tag LazyRow, two LazyVerticalGrids nope, just one LazyVerticalGrid for the capture tiles. The only delta: the Scan hero composable's padding constants come back to v4 values, capture-tile aspect ratio becomes 1.1f, and the sheet's top inset shrinks from 78 to 70 dp. iOS mirror uses the same dimensions on the SwiftUI side. No SQL or nav graph changes.
